How ICM is Calculated

For N players, there are N! = X possible finishing orders.

For EACH possible order:

  1. Calculate probability of that exact finishing order:
    Probability = (stackP1/total) × (stackP2/(total-P1)) × ...
  2. For each player, add: probability × their_payout_in_that_order to their running ICM total

After ALL orders are processed:

  • Each player's ICM = sum of all their contributions
  • Total of all ICM values = Total payout pool
